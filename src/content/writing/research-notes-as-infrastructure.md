## Notes should reduce future friction

A useful note is not a transcript of what was read. It records the claim, the evidence, the uncertainty, and the next question in a form that future work can reuse.

## Connect ideas to decisions

Tags help with discovery, but links between ideas, experiments, and outcomes are what turn a collection of notes into research infrastructure.

### Keep the smallest reusable unit

Short notes are easier to connect and revise. Longer essays can then assemble those units around a specific argument.

## From note to experiment

The final test of a research note is whether it changes what you build, measure, or question next.
